volkit · fx volatility

    Strike takes a number, ATM, or a delta — 25d, 10dp, -25d. A bare 25d takes its wing from the option type. Expiry takes a date or a tenor (1M, 3M), resolved through spot and the delivery date on the pair's holiday calendar. Fwd pts are added to spot as points / pip. Premiums are per unit of base notional in the term currency, undiscounted — this model carries no rate curve. Amounts scale by the notional in millions.
    Product: vanilla, digital (European, pays at expiry), one_touch / no_touch (continuous monitoring; price is a fraction of the payout, so Notional is the payout in millions). Digital ramp % is the call-spread width used to replicate the digital — zero is the unhedgeable limit, wider is what you can actually run. Overhedge shifts the barrier: extend in parallel for the whole life, bend_front full at inception tapering to nothing at expiry, bend_back the reverse. A bent barrier is time-dependent and has no closed form, so it is simulated and reports its Monte Carlo standard error. Buffers are applied against you: selling gets the conservative shift, buying the other way. Leave Spot blank to take spot and interpolated forward points from the feed.
